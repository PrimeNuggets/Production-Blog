Jumping now checks the angle of both feet when landing. There is no feedback yet, but the current thresholds are: Perfect, Good, Ok, & Bad.

The limits on each threshold can be modified on the player object, but currently:

- Perfect: Angle Diff +/-10
- Good: Angle Diff +/-25
- Ok: Angle Diff +/-50
- Bad: Angle Diff +/-180
