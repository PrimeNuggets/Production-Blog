The desert dunes have been populated with props. We have multiple generators for different prop generation styles! Shown here are two - the squares are using our area of interest generator, which spreads points of interest evenly across the map, ensuring no two generate too closely together. The dots are our terrain feature generators, useful for things like plants or ruins that can spawn anywhere, but just don't want to overlap. The prop generation process has been optimized to run fairly quickly to keep level load times down.
