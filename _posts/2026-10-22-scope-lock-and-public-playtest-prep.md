We met yesterday to plan the next few weeks leading up to the International Game Day event at the library on November 17, where we’ll have people try out Lizard Wizard in person.

The board below lays out our current plan. We’re keeping Week 4 mostly open as a buffer in case tasks carry over. Life happens, and some work will probably shift from left to right as we go.

We also need to figure out onboarding for the playtest. That might mean a short, linear tutorial level or contextual pop-ups that introduce the controls and mechanics during gameplay.

Regardless of how the schedule shifts, November 13–16 is reserved for debugging, performance improvements, and build testing. Having fresh eyes on the game should help us see what clicks with players and where we still have work to do.
