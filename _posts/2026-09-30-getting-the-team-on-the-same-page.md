Today we worked through merges and some conflicts, discussed Meeting Minutes, and brought our personal branches up to date with main. We recovered damaged files, shared the fixes, and clarified how everyone should pull updates, merge them into their branch, and push their work.

This helped us coordinate our Git workflow and get back to working from a shared project version.

We also discussed the first lizard wizard sketches, exploring the character’s hat, staff, expressions and game tone, as we develop the prototype’s visual style. Hopefully I can begin work on the 3D model this weekend and manage to pull off an animated rig for Milestone 1.
